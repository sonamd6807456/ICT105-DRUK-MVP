# Acquisition Metrics

## Purpose

Define how the Smart Campus Event Hub team measures early user awareness, student interest, and prototype engagement. These metrics evaluate whether students are discovering the platform, interacting with the landing page, testing the prototype, and showing interest in participating in campus activities.

## Required Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric Name | What It Measures | Formula / Counting Method | Data Source | Target / Success Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M-01 | Landing Page Views | Number of students who visited the Smart Campus Event Hub landing page through different promotion channels | Count total landing page views from Class Chat, QR Poster, Instagram Story, Student Club Promotion, Campus Orientation, and other channels | Acquisition tracking data / landing page analytics | Achieve at least 100 landing page views during the validation period |
| M-02 | CTA Clicks | Number of students who clicked important actions such as Explore Events, Try Prototype, or Join Tester | CTA clicks ÷ landing page views × 100 | Acquisition tracking data / landing page interaction logs | Achieve at least 30% click-through rate |
| M-03 | Prototype Demo Attempts | Number of students who opened and tested the Smart Campus Event Hub prototype | Count users who accessed the prototype and tested features such as event browsing, search, registration, and favorites | Prototype testing records / usability testing dataset | Achieve at least 50 prototype demo attempts |
| M-04 | Feedback Responses | Number of students, organizers, and administrators who provided feedback about the prototype | Count completed feedback forms and usability testing responses | Google Forms / user testing dataset | Collect at least 20 feedback responses |
| M-05 | Interest Conversion | Percentage of visitors who became testers or showed interest in using the Smart Campus Event Hub | Signups or testers ÷ landing page views × 100 | Acquisition tracking data / tester records | Achieve at least 20% conversion rate |

## Interpretation

If acquisition metrics are weak, the Smart Campus Event Hub team will:

- Improve the landing page headline to clearly explain the benefit of finding campus events in one platform.
- Make CTA buttons clearer, such as **"Explore Campus Events"**, **"Register for Events"**, or **"Join as Tester"**.
- Promote the platform through more relevant student channels:
  - Class group chats
  - QR posters around campus
  - Instagram stories
  - Student clubs
  - University orientation activities
  - Campus announcements
- Simplify the prototype flow so students can quickly:
  - Discover events
  - Search by category
  - View event details
  - Register for activities
  - Save favorite events
- Improve the value statement by highlighting:
  - Centralized campus event information
  - Easier event discovery
  - Simple registration process
  - Personalized event tracking

## Expected Outcome

Successful acquisition metrics will show that students are aware of the Smart Campus Event Hub, understand its value, and are willing to use the platform to discover and participate in university events.
