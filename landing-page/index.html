The Problem

Students miss opportunities because campus events are scattered.

University announcements are often shared through different platforms, making it difficult for students to discover upcoming events.

Students need a simple way to find events, understand event information, and register without searching through multiple sources.

Our Solution

One platform for smarter campus participation.

Smart Campus Event Hub provides a centralized platform where students can browse campus events, view details, register for activities, and manage their participation while administrators can organize events efficiently.
